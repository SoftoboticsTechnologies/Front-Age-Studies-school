“KNOWLEDGE IS OUR WEAPON”

We are here to help parents raise happy and healthy children

25 years ago, in 1998, a fairly young couple saw a dream together. A dream to shape the young minds in a way that they grow into becoming independent, empowered individuals with compassion towards others and aspirations to make a mark in the world. The dream manifested in the form of FRONT – AGE STUDIES, a temple of learning with the motto “KNOWLEDGE IS OUR WEAPON”. What started in a one room setting has now grown into an enormous citadel of learning with a junior and a now a senior branch. Set in a pristine, lush green environment, the students here are provided a world class learning environment and are nurtured with the time tested values and ethos of life.
