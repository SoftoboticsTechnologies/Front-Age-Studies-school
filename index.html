At FRONT-AGE STUDIES, we have always believed that both teacher and parents play an important role in the growth and development of a child and it is only through open communications, fruitful discussions and active participation that we can together lay a strong foundation for our children. The FRONT-AGE STUDIES Diary is one of the ways we use to bridge the communication gap between the school and the parents. Not only will this diary act as a handbook for parents, giving you instant answers for most of your questions, it will also establish a valuable liaison between teacher and parents with regard to the quality and quantity of work being attempted by the child in the school and at home. You will also find various forms for different purposes at the back of the diary, which would help parents and the school to actively interact with each other.

About us

We are here to help parents raise happy and healthy children

25 years ago, in 1998, a fairly young couple saw a dream together. A dream to shape the young minds in a way that they grow into becoming independent, empowered individuals with compassion towards others and aspirations to make a mark in the world. The dream manifested in the form of FRONT – AGE STUDIES, a temple of learning with the motto “KNOWLEDGE IS OUR WEAPON”. What started in a one room setting has now grown into an enormous citadel of learning with a junior and a now a senior branch. Set in a pristine, lush green environment, the students here are provided a world class learning environment and are nurtured with the time tested values and ethos of life.
